Food Tracking Analytics

Last refreshed 48 seconds ago

A personal health analytics project that tracks food consumption and correlates ingredients, allergens, and bioactive compounds with health outcomes (sleep quality, mental state, stomach health, finger swelling).

How it works:

  1. Log your meals via the Google Form
  2. Find your user ID below (first 12 characters of your hashed email)
  3. Click on your user to explore AI extended ingredients, allergens, and compounds correlated with your health scores
